About me

Design
with a
point of
view.

I'm Peter — a designer and creative consultant based in Delft, Netherlands. I started Peter Kamm Lab to do design work I actually believe in: considered, intentional, and built to last.

My background spans brand identity, digital design, and strategic consultancy. I've worked with startups finding their voice, cultural organisations redefining themselves, and scale-ups building design culture from scratch.

I work with a small network of trusted collaborators — developers, writers, photographers — so you get boutique attention with full-service output.
